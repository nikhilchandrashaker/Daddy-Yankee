Robustness checks

Peak position drifts later-is-worse (Spearman rho 0.356, p=0.0005; bootstrap interval for the yearly slope 0.236 to 1.146 places). Dropping 2022 weakens it to rho 0.211 (p=0.0621), so part of the drift comes from a cluster of low-peaking Legendaddy-era tracks.
